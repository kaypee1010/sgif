Quiz Control

Turn any quiz on or off, live. A deactivated quiz stops accepting new answers on connected devices within a few seconds — it does not discard or reject anything a student has already submitted or is in the middle of submitting.

⚠️
This is a kill switch, not registration control. It has nothing to do with the Activities sheet's Status column or seat capacity — it only controls whether a running quiz page keeps accepting answers. Use it when something needs to stop right now (wrong question bank, a leak, a technical issue) — not for normal open/close scheduling, which the Activities tab already handles via Reg Start/End and Event Start/End.
